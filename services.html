RCD Testing

We test your RCD safety switches to ensure they’re working correctly and will trip when needed. These devices are essential for protecting against electric shock by cutting power instantly when a fault is detected.

Our RCD testing service includes checking of RCD, proper documentation, and compliance reporting. We ensure your safety systems are reliable and meet current standards, giving you confidence your workplace is protected.
