About Us

Our Work Process

How our Test and Tagging works

We test and tag your equipment to ensure safety, compliance with Australian standards, and reduce electrical hazards—done after hours to minimize disruption.

  • Inspection

    Each electrical item is visually inspected for any signs of damage or wear.

  • Testing

    We use advanced equipment to test for electrical faults and ensure safety compliance with Australian standards.

  • Tagging

    A durable tag is attached to each item, detailing the test date and next due date for re-testing.

  • Reporting

    We provide a comprehensive report, documenting the results and confirming compliance for your records.
